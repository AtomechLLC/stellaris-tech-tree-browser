<!-- Format facts below were measured byte-for-byte from the user's real
     user_empire_designs_v3.4.txt during planning (cat -A on entry 1 and on the
     entries carrying the optional fields). Treat them as the spec. -->

- Indentation is TABS, one per nesting depth. Line terminator is CRLF throughout the real file.
- Top-level entry shape: a line `"<rawName>"=`, then `{` alone on the next line at depth 0, fields at
  depth 1, then `}` alone at depth 0. Entries are separated by exactly one line terminator.
- Block field shape at depth D: `field=` on its own line at depth D, `{` alone at depth D, contents at
  depth D+1, `}` alone at depth D.
- Quoted scalars: `field="value"`. Unquoted scalars: booleans as `yes`/`no` (`is_nomadic=no`,
  `spawn_enabled=yes`, `use_full_regnal_name=yes`), numbers bare (`texture=2`, `clothes=0`), and the
  gender enum bare (`gender=not_set`, `gender=female`).
- Repeated scalar keys (NOT lists): `trait="..."` inside `species`, and `ethic="..."` at entry level.
  394 `ethic=` occurrences across the real file, zero `ethics=` blocks.
- Bracketed string lists (`civics`, `empire_flag.colors`): block form with one quoted value per line at depth+1.
- `variables` array-of-objects formatting (exact, from entry 1): after the opening `{` of the variables
  block at depth D, emit ONE line containing only (D+1) tabs, then for each element an object block at
  depth D+1, and after EACH element a line containing a single space character (0x20) and nothing else,
  then the closing `}` at depth D.
- Top-level field emission order (measured): key, ship_prefix, species, secondary_species?, name, adjective,
  authority, government, is_nomadic, advisor_voice_type?, planet_name, planet_class, system_name,
  initializer, graphical_culture, city_graphical_culture, empire_flag, ruler, spawn_as_fallen,
  ignore_portrait_duplication, room, spawn_enabled, ethic (repeated), civics, origin.
- `species` block field order: class, portrait, species_name, species_plural, species_adjective,
  name_list, gender, trait (repeated).
- `ruler` block field order: gender, name (containing full_names + use_full_regnal_name), portrait,
  texture, evolution_mask, attachment, clothes, trait, leader_class.
- `empire_flag` block order: icon (category, file), background (category, file), colors (6 quoted values,
  unused slots are the literal string "null"). A save's `country.flag.colors` can carry only 4 entries —
  padding to 6 is this serializer's job.
- Contract from 04-01 (`app/src/lib/empire/designSchema.ts`): `DesignEntry`, `DesignSpecies`, `DesignRuler`,
  `DesignFlag`, `LocName` (`{ key, literal?, variables? }`), `LocVariable` (`{ key: string, value: LocName }`).
- Contract from 04-01 (`app/src/lib/empire/designsText.ts`): `findTopLevelSpans(text): EntrySpan[]`,
  `spliceDesignsFile(file, removedIndices, addedTexts)` — `addedTexts` entries are exactly what this
  serializer returns (no trailing separator; the splice adds it).
